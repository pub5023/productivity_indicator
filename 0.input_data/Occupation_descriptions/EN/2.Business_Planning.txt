# Planning and Marketing Roles

Characteristics: Their day-to-day work involves organizing market and customer information, creating planning proposals and instructions for initiatives, and preparing presentation materials for internal and external stakeholders.

Examples:
- Business Planning
- Product Planning
- Marketing Specialist
- Public Relations / PR Specialist

-----------------------

# Key Tasks

#1 Article Summarization: Information Gathering and Input Organization

Planning and marketing professionals need to organize market information, customer trends, and competitive intelligence required for decision-making in a short period of time, and restructure it into a form that can be used for planning and marketing initiatives.

## Specific Work Activities

- Read industry news, research reports, social media trends, customer surveys, and other materials; extract only the parts relevant to the company’s planning activities; and summarize them into one to two pages.
- Research competitors’ new products, campaigns, and advertising initiatives, and organize their strengths, weaknesses, and points of differentiation.
- Summarize data such as market size, growth rates, and target customer attributes in a concise format while visualizing them in graphs.
- Read long research documents of 100 to 200 pages and restructure them into key-point materials that can be used in planning meetings.
- Organize information into the structure of “Conclusion → Evidence → Insights / Implications,” leaving only the points directly linked to the direction of the plan.

---

#2 Creating Instruction Documents: Design and Guidance for Planning Execution

Planning and marketing professionals create documents that clearly communicate to internal and external stakeholders “what should be executed, by when, and how.”

## Specific Work Activities

- Organize the intent and target audience of a new product plan, and define them in text and diagrams as a planning brief.
- Organize current customer behavior and purchasing processes, and create a customer journey map that visualizes key issues.
- Clarify the objectives and KPIs of the plan, and organize the priorities and execution steps of each initiative in bullet points.
- Create creative briefs for advertising agencies and production companies, including tone and manner, messaging, and material requirements.
- Break down project tasks and list schedules and owners as a WBS, or Work Breakdown Structure.
- Organize the roles of related departments, such as Sales, Development, and Customer Success, and clarify responsibilities using a RACI matrix.
- Describe tasks to be requested from internal members in the format of “who does what by when” as a procedure manual.

---

#3 Creating Presentation Materials: Planning Proposals and Reports for Internal and External Stakeholders

Because the deliverables of planning and marketing roles are ultimately presented as slide decks to executives, sales departments, and external partners, creating presentation materials is an extremely important task.

## Specific Work Activities

- Build a logical storyline based on the background, issues, insights, and proposed plan: background → issue → implications → proposal → impact.
- Create slides for executives that are conclusion-first, with one message per slide.
- Visualize research data and the effects of initiatives in graphs, and clearly state the “So What,” or what matters most, on each slide.
- Translate the proposed plan into concrete execution steps, required resources, budget, and expected impact, organizing it in a form that makes decision-making easier.
- Adjust expressions, color schemes, and wording to match the company culture and executives’ preferences.
- Before the presentation, prepare anticipated questions and draft answers, or Q&A materials, and review them with supervisors and relevant stakeholders.
- Practice the speaking order, key points to emphasize, and timing of slide transitions in preparation for the presentation.
