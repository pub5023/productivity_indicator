# Customer Support and Technical Support Roles

Characteristics: Their day-to-day work involves providing real-time diagnosis and solutions through chat systems in response to technical inquiries from customers.

Examples:
- Technical Support Agent
- Help Desk Representative
- Customer Success Representative
- Chat Support Operator

-----------------------

# Key Tasks

#1 Diagnosing Customer Issues and Gathering Information: Interviews and Situation Assessment

Support professionals identify the root causes of technical issues based on vague customer inquiries submitted through chat.

## Specific Work Activities

- Ask follow-up questions to clarify specific symptoms and error messages from customer statements such as “It doesn’t work” or “I’m getting an error.”
- Check the customer’s contract status and system environment, and isolate the issue.
- Refer to past similar cases and troubleshooting guides, or manuals, to find applicable cases.
- Form a hypothesis about the most likely cause from among several possibilities.

---

#2 Proposing Solutions and Drafting Responses: Providing Solutions

Based on the diagnosis, support professionals present clear solution steps that customers can follow.

## Specific Work Activities

- Avoid technical jargon and explain procedures in plain language that even beginners can understand.
- Find links to relevant technical documents and provide them at the appropriate timing.
- Review the content of answer suggestions generated by AI, determine whether they are correct, and send them to the customer.
- Monitor the customer’s reactions, or sentiment, and add empathetic or apologetic wording to reduce stress.

---

#3 Accumulating Knowledge and Learning: Organizational Knowledge Sharing

Support professionals accumulate insights gained from individual interactions and turn them into assets for both personal skill development and the team.

## Specific Work Activities

- Remember successful resolution patterns so that similar cases can be handled quickly in the future.
- New operators learn appropriate wording and diagnostic flows by observing “expert response patterns” suggested by AI.
- When system failures or unknown errors occur, escalate or report them to supervisors or development teams.
- Maintain quality while handling multiple chats at the same time through multitasking.
