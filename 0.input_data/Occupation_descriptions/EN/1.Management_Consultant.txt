# Consulting-Related Occupations

Characteristics: Their day-to-day work involves summarizing client information, creating instruction documents such as To-Be processes and requirements definitions, and preparing presentation materials.

Examples:
- Management Consultant
- IT Consultant
- Business Process Improvement Consultant
- Strategy Analyst

-----------------------

## Key Tasks

#1 Article Summarization: Information Gathering and Input Organization

Management consultants need to understand large volumes of information in a short period of time and restructure it into a form that can be used for decision-making.

## Specific Work Activities

- Read industry news, research reports, government statistics, and other materials; extract only the parts relevant to the client; and condense them into one to two pages.
- Organize competitors’ activities and summarize the key points from the perspectives of threats and opportunities.
- Convert figures such as market size, growth rates, and customer needs into concise summaries while visualizing them in graphs.
- Read 100- to 300-page documents and restructure them into executive-level summary materials that management can understand in five minutes.
- Organize information into the structure of “Conclusion → Evidence → Implications,” leaving only the points necessary for decision-making.

---

#2 Creating Instruction Documents: Design and Guidance for Project Execution

Consultants clearly document “what needs to be done, by when, and how” in order to move projects forward.

## Specific Work Activities

- Organize the client’s issues and define the desired future state, or To-Be state, using text and diagrams.
- Conduct interviews to understand the current business process flow, or As-Is state, and visualize it while marking issues in red.
- Analyze the gaps between the As-Is and To-Be states and organize the points for improvement in bullet points.
- Create requirements definition documents, including functional and non-functional requirements, for system implementation or business process improvement.
- Break down project tasks and list them as a WBS, or Work Breakdown Structure.
- Organize stakeholder roles and clarify them in a RACI matrix, or responsibility assignment matrix.
- Describe the tasks to be requested from client representatives in the format of “who does what by when” as a procedure manual.

---

#3 Creating Presentation Materials: Client Reporting and Proposals

Because consultants’ deliverables are ultimately presented as slide decks, creating presentation materials is one of their most important tasks.

## Specific Work Activities

- Build a logical storyline based on research findings: introduction → analysis → implications → proposal.
- Create slides for executives that are conclusion-first, with one message per slide.
- Visualize data in graphs and clearly state the “So What” on each slide.
- Translate improvement plans and strategic proposals into concrete execution steps, required resources, and estimated impact.
- Adjust expressions, color schemes, and wording to match the characteristics of the client’s industry.
- Before the presentation, prepare anticipated questions and draft answers, or Q&A materials, and review them within the team.
- Practice the speaking order, key points to emphasize, and timing of slide transitions in preparation for the reporting meeting.
