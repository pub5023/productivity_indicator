# Project Manager Roles

Characteristics: Their day-to-day work involves managing project progress, organizing requirements, coordinating stakeholders, and creating instruction documents and reporting materials to complete deliverables within deadlines.

Examples:
- IT Project Manager
- System Implementation Project Manager
- Web / App Development Project Manager
- Business Transformation Project Manager

-----------------------

# Key Tasks

---

#1 Article Summarization: Information Gathering and Input Organization

Project managers need to organize information required for project decision-making in a short period of time and restructure it into a form that stakeholders can easily understand.

## Specific Work Activities

- Read technology trends, industry news, and vendor materials related to the project; extract only the parts that affect the project; and summarize them into one to two pages.
- Research success and failure cases from similar projects, and organize risks, success factors, and applicable points.
- Summarize data related to system requirements and business requirements, such as market size, number of users, and expected system load, in a concise format while visualizing them in graphs.
- Read long documents provided by clients or internal teams, such as requirements documents, specifications, and research materials, and restructure them into key-point materials that project members can quickly understand.
- Organize information into the structure of “Conclusion → Evidence → Implications for the Project,” leaving only the points necessary for decision-making.

---

#2 Creating Instruction Documents: Design and Guidance for Project Execution

Project managers clearly document “what needs to be done, by whom, by when, and how” in order to move projects forward smoothly.

## Specific Work Activities

- Organize client requirements and clarify them in text and diagrams as a requirements definition document, including functional and non-functional requirements.
- Conduct interviews to understand the current business process flow, or As-Is state, and visualize it while marking issues in red.
- Create the To-Be business process flow, and organize improvement points and required system functions in bullet points.
- Break down project tasks and list schedules and owners as a WBS, or Work Breakdown Structure.
- Organize the roles of stakeholders, such as Development, Infrastructure, QA, and the client, and clarify responsibilities using a RACI matrix.
- Create detailed work instructions for vendors and development teams, including specifications, deadlines, and deliverable formats.
- Create a risk and issue management log, and operate it as a management document that clearly defines countermeasures, owners, and deadlines.

---

#3 Creating Presentation Materials: Client Reporting and Proposals

Project managers create clear presentation materials to explain project progress, issues, and countermeasures to clients and executives.

## Specific Work Activities

- Build a logical storyline based on the project background, objectives, progress, and issues: background → current status → issues → countermeasures → next steps.
- Create slides for executives that are conclusion-first, with one message per slide.
- Visualize progress rates, delay status, risk status, and other information in graphs, and clearly state the “So What,” or what matters most, on each slide.
- Translate countermeasures for issues into concrete execution steps, required resources, and scope of impact, organizing them in a form that makes decision-making easier.
- Adjust expressions, color schemes, and wording to match the client’s culture and decision-making style.
- Before the reporting meeting, prepare anticipated questions and draft answers, or Q&A materials, and review them within the team.
- Practice the speaking order, key points to emphasize, and timing of slide transitions in preparation for the presentation.
