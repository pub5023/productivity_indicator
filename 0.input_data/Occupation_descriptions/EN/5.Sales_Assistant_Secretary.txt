# Administrative and Assistant Roles

Characteristics: These roles are responsible for creating documents such as meeting minutes, summaries, instruction documents, and internal explanatory materials, as well as organizing information to help the organization operate smoothly.

Examples:
- Sales Assistant
- Executive Assistant / Secretary
- General Affairs / Administration Department

-----------------------

# Key Tasks

---

#1 Article Summarization: Information Gathering and Input Organization

Administrative and assistant roles are responsible for organizing information needed for internal decision-making and operational efficiency, and summarizing it in a form that stakeholders can quickly understand.

## Specific Work Activities

- Read internal and external news, industry information, internal announcements, and other materials; extract only the parts needed by relevant departments; and summarize them into one to two pages.
- Read meeting materials and meeting minutes, and create summaries that organize important decisions, action items, and deadlines.
- Check information on internal rules and procedural changes, summarize the key points, and restructure them into short explanatory texts that can be shared with stakeholders.
- Read long reports and project materials, and summarize them into key-point materials that supervisors can understand in a short period of time.
- Organize information into the structure of “Conclusion → Background → Required Actions,” leaving only the points directly connected to business operations.

---

#2 Creating Instruction Documents: Design and Guidance for Business Execution

Administrative and assistant roles create procedure manuals, request documents, and internal explanatory texts to help internal operations run smoothly.

## Specific Work Activities

- Organize requests from supervisors and departments, and clearly describe the purpose, procedure, and deadline in a work instruction document.
- Organize the flow of internal procedures, such as applications, approvals, and office supply management, and visualize them in procedure manuals using diagrams and bullet points.
- Create meeting agendas and facilitation schedules, and clarify what stakeholders need to prepare.
- Organize project tasks and list schedules and owners as a WBS, or Work Breakdown Structure.
- Organize the roles of related departments and clarify responsibilities using a RACI matrix, or responsibility assignment matrix.
- Describe requests to external partners and internal members in emails or documents in the format of “who does what by when.”
- Summarize internal rules and operational changes, and explain them clearly as internal announcements.

---

#3 Creating Presentation Materials: Internal Explanations and Reports

Administrative and assistant roles are expected to create clear materials for internal meetings and reporting.

## Specific Work Activities

- Organize the meeting objective, background, current status, and issues, and build a logical storyline: background → current status → issues → countermeasures → next steps.
- Create slides for supervisors and related departments that are conclusion-first, with one message per slide.
- Visualize operational data, such as case numbers, progress, and costs, in graphs, and clearly state the “So What,” or what matters most, on each slide.
- Translate operational improvement ideas and proposals into concrete execution steps, required resources, and scope of impact, organizing them in a form that makes decision-making easier.
- Adjust expressions, color schemes, and wording to match the company culture and supervisors’ preferences.
- Before the presentation, prepare anticipated questions and draft answers, or Q&A materials, and review them with supervisors.
- Practice the speaking order, key points to emphasize, and timing of slide transitions in preparation for the presentation.
